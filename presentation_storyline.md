
# PharmEasy Regional Pulse – Presentation Storyline

## 1. Executive Storyline (SCR)

### Situation
The PharmEasy Regional Pulse dataset contains 2,100 cleaned orders across the April–June period. The dashboard helps compare sales, profit, order volume, and category performance across regions.

### Complication
Sales performance differs across regions. Hyderabad recorded the highest total sales at ₹7,47,583.99. Guntur also showed a notable month-to-month change: its sales increased by 122.19% from April to May. This increase is worth investigating to understand whether it reflects a sustained trend or a temporary change.

### Resolution
The interactive dashboard allows users to filter by region and examine monthly sales, regional sales, category contribution, and key performance indicators. These views help managers identify changes, compare regions, and decide where further investigation may be useful.

## 2. Regional Manager Storyline: Guntur (OCD)

### Overview
Guntur's sales increased by 122.19%, from ₹62,442.27 in April to ₹1,38,738.93 in May 2026. This was a substantial month-to-month change that requires further review.

### Category
Wellness & Nutrition recorded the largest absolute sales increase, rising from ₹19,297.59 in April to ₹53,085.01 in May, an increase of ₹33,787.42. Medical Devices recorded the second-largest increase, rising by ₹23,276.06 over the same period. These two categories accounted for the largest category-level increases in Guntur.

### Detail
Guntur's order count increased from 51 in April to 77 in May. In June, sales were ₹99,745.18 across 62 orders. These figures are based on the cleaned order dataset and SQL monthly sales results. The data shows which categories increased, but it does not establish why they increased.

### Suggested Action
Review the order records and relevant business factors for Wellness & Nutrition and Medical Devices before the next monthly performance discussion. Use the dashboard to compare category and monthly trends before making operational decisions.

## 3. Stakeholder Questions and Answers

### Q1. Executive: How do we know the Guntur increase is reliable?

**Answer:** I understand the concern about whether such a large increase is based on a consistent calculation. The SQL results confirm that Guntur's sales increased from ₹62,442.27 in April to ₹1,38,738.93 in May, a rise of 122.19%. The order count also increased from 51 to 77. However, these figures do not explain the business reason behind the increase.

To address any remaining concerns about the underlying records, review Guntur's April and May order-level data and category totals before the next monthly performance discussion.

### Q2. Regional Manager: Why did Guntur's sales increase so much from April to May?

**Answer:** I understand that the 122.19% increase raises questions about what drove the change. The data confirms that Wellness & Nutrition had the largest absolute category-level increase, of ₹33,787.42, followed by Medical Devices at ₹23,276.06. However, the figures do not establish whether promotions, demand, or other business factors caused these increases.

To investigate further, review the order records for these two categories and check relevant business information before the next monthly performance discussion.

### Q3. Executive: Did Guntur maintain its May sales level in June?

**Answer:** I understand the concern about whether the May increase was sustained. The data confirms that Guntur's sales declined by 28.11% from May to June, from ₹1,38,738.93 to ₹99,745.18. Order count also fell from 77 to 62. However, the available figures do not establish why sales declined.

To resolve this, compare Guntur's category-level sales and order trends across all three months, and review relevant business factors before the next monthly performance discussion.
